# ENTITY RELATIONSHIP DIAGRAM (ER DIAGRAM)

## 1. Project Title
Last-Mile Delivery Operations Analytics

## 2. Entities

### CUSTOMERS
- customer_id (Primary Key)
- customer_name
- customer_type
- city
- phone

### ORDERS
- order_id (Primary Key)
- customer_id (Foreign Key)
- order_date
- service_type
- priority
- order_value

### DRIVERS
- driver_id (Primary Key)
- driver_name
- phone
- city

### VEHICLES
- vehicle_id (Primary Key)
- vehicle_type
- registration_number

### DELIVERIES
- delivery_id (Primary Key)
- order_id (Foreign Key)
- driver_id (Foreign Key)
- vehicle_id (Foreign Key)
- delivery_status
- delivery_minutes
- attempts

## 4. Relationships

1. One customer can place multiple orders.
2. One order has one delivery.
3. One driver can handle multiple deliveries.
4. One vehicle can be used for multiple deliveries.

## 5. Keys

Primary Keys:
- customers.customer_id
- orders.order_id
- drivers.driver_id
- vehicles.vehicle_id
- deliveries.delivery_id

Foreign Keys:
- orders.customer_id
- deliveries.order_id
- deliveries.driver_id
- deliveries.vehicle_id

## 6. Conclusion

The ER diagram represents the relationships between customers, orders, drivers, vehicles, and deliveries.

It helps understand how the five tables are connected in the Last-Mile Delivery Operations Analytics database.
